<p class="theme-question">How do relativistic outflows reveal their engines and environments?</p>
<p class="theme-intro">The deaths of massive stars and the mergers of compact objects launch relativistic jets and engine-powered transients. I model their central engines (including magnetars) and multiwavelength afterglows, and bring that modelling to observational teams chasing the universe&#x27;s fastest transients — gamma-ray bursts, fast X-ray transients, fast radio bursts, dirty fireballs, and engine-driven supernovae — connecting theory to real data from facilities such as the Einstein Probe, FAST, Chandra, and the VLA.</p>
