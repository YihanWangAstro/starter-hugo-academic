<p class="theme-question">How do stellar flybys reshape planetary systems and set planets adrift?</p>
<p class="theme-intro">Most planetary systems are born in clustered environments where encounters with passing stars are common. With high-precision few-body simulations I study how flybys reshape planetary architectures — forming hot Jupiters, ejecting free-floating and binary planets, and swapping giant planets between stars — connecting cluster dynamics to the diversity of observed exoplanets.</p>
